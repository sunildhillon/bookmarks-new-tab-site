Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Bookmarks New Tab is a Chrome extension. It has no server of its own, and its developer does not receive or have access to your data. It contains no analytics or advertising.

Google Tasks

Google Tasks is optional. If you connect it, the extension asks for permission to view and edit your tasks and task lists (the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks).

  • Task data is exchanged only between your browser and Google, to show your tasks and save the changes you make.
  • A copy of your last loaded tasks is kept in your browser so they can be shown offline. Choosing "Disconnect Google" in the Tasks menu removes it.
  • Task data is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

The extension's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

You can remove the extension's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Data stored on your computer

Your bookmarks are read and changed through Chrome. Settings, tags, pins and notes are stored in your browser. Chrome Sync may copy some settings to your Google account if you use it.

If you choose a folder for notes and backups, the extension writes files there. If that folder is synced by a service such as OneDrive, that service's own terms apply.

Text saved from web pages

When you select text on a page and choose "Save to Notes", the extension saves that text with the page's address, title and the time. It reads a page only when you do this.
